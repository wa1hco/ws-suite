[[MENU_FILTERS]]
=== Filters

image::menu-Filters.png[Filters menu]

Quick filters for the Band Activity pane. The lists they use (Ignore
List, Whitelist, Territories) are set on menu:File[Settings > Filters],
see <<SETTINGS_FILTERS>>. All filters can be combined. The *BP* checkbox
on the main window bypasses them with one click. While a *Hide* filter
is on, the status label turns cyan and reads "Receiving, Filters On"
(green "Receiving, Filters bypassed" with *BP*).

[cols="2,5",options="header"]
|===
|Menu item |What it does

|*Hide stations worked before on band*
|Hides messages from calls already in your log on the current band.

|*Hide stations worked today or yesterday*
|Hides calls you logged today (or yesterday). The item reads "... worked
today" unless the matching option on the *Filters* tab adds yesterday.

|*Hide stations from Ignore List*
|Hides calls on the Ignore List. The *Ignore* button adds the DX Call
to that list; erase it from the *File* menu.

|*Ignore stations worked before on band*
|Such stations stay visible, but WS does not answer them automatically
(CQ: First / Max Dist / Max dB / Min dB, Wait and Pounce, Wait and Reply).

|*Ignore stations worked today or yesterday*
|Same, for calls logged today (or yesterday).

|*Ignore stations from Ignore List*
|Same, for calls on the Ignore List.

|*Hide stations from Territory 1* ... *4*
|Hides stations whose country name contains the text entered for that
territory. Once you enter a territory, the item shows its name
(e.g. "Hide stations from Italy").

|*Hide stations from Europe*, *North America*, *South America*, *Asia*,
*Africa*, *Oceania*, *Antarctica*
|Hides all stations from that continent. Handy for showing only DX.

|*Highlight callsigns worked before on band*
|Grey background for calls already worked on this band.

|*Highlight callsigns worked today or yesterday*
|Yellow on dark grey for calls logged today (or yesterday).

|*Highlight callsigns from Ignore List*
|Yellow on dark red for calls on the Ignore List.

|*Highlight stations from Territory 1* ... *4*
|Purple background for calls from that territory.

|*Highlight Whitelist entries*
|Magenta background for calls that contain a Whitelist entry. The
Whitelist filter itself does not have to be on.
|===

[[MENU_DECODE]]
=== Decode

image::menu-Decode.png[Decode menu]

WS shows only the items that apply to the current mode. Some need
*Enable VHF and submode features* (menu:File[Settings > General]).

[cols="2,5",options="header"]
|===
|Menu item |What it does

|*Fast*
|Fastest decoding, lowest sensitivity.

|*Normal*
|Medium depth.

|*Deep*
|Best sensitivity, slower, with a slightly higher chance of false decodes.
Greyed out in Echo, FreqCal and JT65 with VHF features.

|*Use multithreaded FT8 decoder*
|FT8 only. Runs several decoder threads in parallel. On a busy band this
usually gives more decodes. For extreme weak-signal work the
single-threaded *Deep* decoder is often better. Configure it under *Parameters*.

a|*Parameters*

image::menu-Decode-Parameters.png["Decode, Parameters submenu"]
a|Fine tuning for the multithreaded FT8 decoder. It has no effect unless
*Use multithreaded FT8 decoder* is on.

[cols="2,5"]
!===
!*Number of threads* +
image:menu-Decode-Parameters-FT8_threads.png[Number of threads]
!*Auto* (default) leaves some cores free: with 2-4 logical cores it
uses one fewer, with 5-8 two fewer, with 9-15 three fewer, and with 16
or more it uses 12. *1*-*12* set the count directly, capped at the number of cores.

!*Decoding cycles* +
image:menu-Decode-Parameters-Decoding_cycles.png[Decoding cycles]
!*1*, *2* or *3* (default) cycles of 3 passes each, so 3, 6 or 9 passes.
More cycles find more weak signals but take longer. Early decodes (see
*Decoder start*) always use 2 cycles.

!*QSO RX freq sensitivity* +
image:menu-Decode-Parameters-QSO_RX_freq_sensitivity.png[QSO RX freq sensitivity]
!*Low*, *Medium* (default), *High*. Threshold for a special decoder
that looks for the expected messages from the DX Call at your Rx
frequency during a QSO. Higher gives more decodes but also more false
ones. In Hound mode *Low* is always used.

!*Decoder sensitivity* +
image:menu-Decode-Parameters-Decoder_sensitivity.png[Decoder sensitivity]
!*Minimum*: normal sync thresholds. *Use low thresholds*: lower sync
thresholds, so more candidates are tried. *Use subpass* (default): low
thresholds plus extra weak-signal subpasses. More sensitive also means more CPU load.

!*Decoder start* +
image:menu-Decode-Parameters-Decoder_start.png[Decoder start]
!When decoding starts in the 15 s period.
*2-Stage*: early decode at about 11.8 s, final decode at 14.1 s.
*3-Stage*: at about 11.8, 13.2 and 14.4 s.
*Early*: one decode at 13.8 s. *Normal* (default): 14.1 s.
*Late*: 14.4 s. The staged settings show strong signals sooner.

!*Wideband DX Call search*
!On (default): AP search for the DX Call's messages across the whole
passband. Off: only in a narrow window around the Rx frequency.

!*Hide FT8 dupe messages*
!On (default): if the threads decode the same message twice in one
period, it is shown once, with the stronger report.

!*Skip a8 decodes*
!Turns off the "a8" attempt: a decode at the Rx frequency of the
expected message from the DX Call, built from *DX Call* and *DX Grid*.
!===

|*Enable averaging*
|JT4, JT65 and Q65 with VHF features. Averages successive periods to
decode signals too weak for one period. The panes become *Single-Period
Decodes* and *Average Decodes*.

|*Enable deep search*
|JT4 and JT65 with VHF features. When normal decoding fails, it
compares the signal with likely messages built from your call, the DX
Call, the DX Grid and `CALL3.TXT`.

|*Enable AP*
|The first of the two items. FT8 only: a priori (AP) decoding, which
uses your call, the DX Call and the QSO state to find weaker messages.
AP decodes are marked `a1` ... `a8`.

|*Enable AP*
|The second item. JT65 with VHF features only: AP decoding for JT65.


|*Auto Clear Avg after decode*
|After a successful decode within *F Tol* of the Rx frequency, clears
the average so the next message starts clean.

|*Accept WS-MAP/EME65 click events*
|Q65 and JT65 with VHF features (default on). Clicking a station in
WS-MAP or EME65 sets the DX Call, grid, frequency and period in WS.
With *Double-click on call sets Tx enable* on, a double-click also starts the QSO. It also
brings in the decodes that EME65 forwards.

|*Claim WS-MAP*
|Shown when click events are accepted. If several WS instances watch
the same WS-MAP/EME65 source, only the instance holding the claim acts
on clicks. A claim is dropped automatically if its instance stops
refreshing it for 10 s. The *Claim WS-MAP* button on the main window does the same.

a|*WS-MAP/EME65 source*

image::menu-Decode-QMAP_source.png[WS-MAP/EME65 source submenu]
a|Which WS-MAP or EME65 instance WS listens to.

* *Auto* (default) and *1*: the default instance (Instance ID 1 or
unset in the WS-MAP settings). Use this with a single WS-MAP.
* *2*, *3*, *4*: only the instance with that Instance ID.

Changing the source releases any claim. The change takes effect at once;
restarting WS afterwards is recommended.

|*Disable clicks on waterfall*
|JT65 and Q65 with VHF features. Protects your Rx/Tx offsets from accidental
clicks on the waterfall. Hold kbd:[Alt] to click anyway.

|*Hide AP information*
|FT8 only. Removes the `a1` ... `a9` and `?` marks from displayed decodes.

|*Reduce false decodes*
|FT8, not in special operating modes. Drops weak decodes that look
implausible, for example odd combinations of `/P`, `/R`, `R`, hashed
calls or contest exchanges at low SNR. These lines are not written to `ALL.TXT`.
With the single-threaded decoder, decodes below -20 dB are also hidden
if the calls are not in `ALLCALL7.TXT` and do not look like valid callsigns.

|*Full Duplex Mode*
|Keeps receiving and decoding while you transmit, e.g. with audio from a
second receiver or a WebSDR. Works for FT8, FT4, Q65, MSK144 and the other modes.
|===

[[MENU_MODE]]
=== Mode

image::menu-Mode.png[Mode menu]

Selects the operating mode. The menu is locked while you transmit. For
details of each mode, see the WSJT-X User Guide.

[cols="2,5",options="header"]
|===
|Menu item |Typical use

|*FST4*
|LF/MF and HF weak-signal QSOs with long periods (15 s to 30 min).

|*FT2*
|Very fast 77-bit mode with 3.75 s periods (half of FT4). Introduced by WSJT-X Improved 3.1.

|*FT4*
|Contests and fast HF QSOs, 7.5 s periods.

|*FT8*
|The everyday HF/6 m mode, 15 s periods. Also DXpedition (Fox/Hound) and SuperFox.

|*JT4*
|Microwave EME and terrestrial paths with Doppler spread.

|*JT9*
|HF/LF weak signals, 1-minute periods.

|*JT65*
|EME on VHF/UHF and classic HF JT65.

|*Q65*
|EME, ionospheric and rain scatter on VHF to microwaves. Submodes A-E,
periods 15-300 s. Used with WS-MAP.

|*MSK144*
|Meteor scatter on 6 m and 2 m.

|*FST4W*
|WSPR-like beacon mode for LF/MF, periods 2-30 min.

|*WSPR*
|Beacon transmissions for propagation studies. Spots go to WSPRnet.

|*Echo*
|EME echo tests: measures your own echoes from the Moon.

|*FreqCal*
|Frequency calibration of your rig. See <<MENU_TOOLS>>.
|===

[[MENU_TOOLS]]
=== Tools

image::menu-Tools.png[Tools menu]

[cols="2,5",options="header"]
|===
|Menu item |What it does

|*Erase reference spectrum*
|Clears the reference spectrum being measured.

|*Measure reference spectrum*
|Starts *Monitor* and averages the received audio spectrum. Click
*Stop* to save it to `refspec.dat`. Check
*Ref Spec* in the Wide Graph to flatten the audio with this filter.

|*Measure phase response*
|Turns MSK144 phase training on or off. WS trains on decodes from the
DX Call and saves the phase-equalization coefficients to a file in the
data directory. Training ends on its own once it has enough data.

|*Equalization tools ...*
|Opens a window that plots the measured, proposed and current phase and
group delay, plus the reference spectrum. Use it to load (*Phase ...*),
refresh or discard phase-equalization data.

|*Execute frequency calibration cycle*
|In FreqCal mode, retunes the rig to the next FreqCal frequency in
menu:File[Settings > Frequencies] at the end of each period, so you can
measure several stations of known frequency in turn.

|*Solve for calibration parameters*
|Fits slope (ppm) and intercept (Hz) to the measurements in `fmt.all`
and shows the result. *Apply* stores them as your calibration in
menu:File[Settings > Frequencies]. `fmt.all` can then be renamed to `fmt.bak`.
|===

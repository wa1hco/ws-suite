[[SETTINGS_GENERAL]]
=== General

image::settings-general.png["Settings, General tab",align="center"]

==== Station Details

[cols="2,5",options="header"]
|===
|Setting |What it does

|*My Call*
|Your callsign.

|*My Grid*
|Your Maidenhead locator, preferably 6 characters (e.g. CM87ux).
Distances and azimuths are calculated from it.

|*AutoGrid*
|Lets external programs (e.g. a GPS helper) change *My Grid*.

|*IARU Region*
|Region 1: Europe, Africa, Middle East, northern Asia. Region 2: the
Americas. Region 3: southern Asia and the Pacific. Selects the matching
default frequencies.

|*Message generation for type 2 compound callsign holders*
|Only for calls with an add-on prefix or suffix that is not in the
short list (menu:Help[List of Type 1 prefixes and suffixes]). Chooses which
generated message (Tx1, Tx3 or Tx6) carries your full call.
|===

==== Display

[cols="2,5",options="header"]
|===
|Setting |What it does

|*Start new period decodes at top*
|Each period starts at the top of Band Activity and does not scroll
away, so you can double-click while decoding is still running.

|*Blank line between decoding periods*
|Separator line between periods in Band Activity.

|*Show blank line with time stamp*
|The separator line also shows the UTC of the period.

|*Display distance in miles*
|Miles instead of kilometres.

|*Highlight DX Call* / *Highlight DX Grid*
|Marks the call and the grid of your current QSO partner in the messages.

|*Tx messages to Rx frequency window*
|Your own transmitted messages also appear in the Rx Frequency pane.

|*Highlight RRR/RR73/73 in messages for us*
|Makes the end of a QSO easy to spot.

|*Show DXCC, grid, and worked-before status*
|Adds the country and the new-DXCC / new-grid / worked-before marks to
decodes. Required for the highlighting on the *Colors* tab.

|*Show principal prefix instead of country name*
|Shorter: "DL" instead of "Fed. Rep. of Germany".

|*Show DXCC for all messages*
|Adds the country to every message, not only to CQ calls.

|*Font...* / *Decoded Text Font...*
|Font for the whole program / for the Band Activity and Rx Frequency panes.
|===

==== Behavior

[cols="2,5",options="header"]
|===
|Setting |What it does

|*Monitor off at startup*
|WS starts without decoding until you click *Monitor*.

|*Monitor returns to last used frequency*
|Switching *Monitor* on returns to the last monitored frequency.
Unchecked, the current rig frequency is kept.

|*Double-click on call sets Tx enable*
|Double-clicking a decode starts transmitting at once.

|*Disable Tx after sending 73*
|*Enable Tx* turns off after a 73 or free-text message.

|*Calling CQ forces Call 1st*
|When you call CQ, *Call 1st* is checked so WS answers the first caller automatically.

|*Alternate F1-F6 bindings*
|Switches the kbd:[F1]–kbd:[F6] shortcuts for the Tx messages to the contest
assignment (see menu:Help[Keyboard shortcuts]).

|*Enable VHF and submode features*
|Shows the VHF/UHF/microwave controls (submodes, averaging, Sh, auto-QSY
and so on). Turn it off on HF: it disables wideband JT65 multi-decoding.

|*Allow Tx frequency changes while transmitting*
|Uncheck if your rig cannot process CAT commands during Tx (often needed with split).

|*Decode after EME delay*
|Starts decoding later in 1-minute modes, so EME signals delayed by
about 2.5 s are included.

|*Single decode*
|For JT4, JT65, Q65 and FST4: decodes only near the Rx frequency
(within *F Tol*) instead of searching the whole passband. In JT4/JT65/Q65
the two panes become *Single-Period Decodes* and *Average Decodes*.

|*Tune watchdog*
|Stops *Tune* after the set number of seconds.

|*MSK144/Q65: Tx until 73 is received*
|Keeps sending until the other station's 73 arrives, at most 10
attempts. Helpful for difficult VHF/UHF/SHF QSOs.

|*Auto-open/close Astronomical data window*
|Opens the Astronomical Data window when you switch to Q65 or JT65 with
*Decode after EME delay* on, and closes it when no longer needed.

|*kHz entry without k (restart required)*
|In the frequency box, "164" followed by kbd:[Enter] means +164 kHz within
the current MHz (438.065 -> 438.164 MHz). Handy for jumping between EME
segments from the numeric keypad.

|*Turn progress bar red while transmitting*
|Makes Tx easy to see at a glance.

|*CW ID after 73* / *ID Interval*
|Sends a CW ID after every 73 or free text / every N minutes (0 = off),
always in the quiet part of the period.

|*Tx watchdog*
|Stops unattended transmissions after the set number of minutes.
|===

==== Additional features

These options come from WSJT-X Improved and are not in WSJT-X.

[cols="2,5",options="header"]
|===
|Setting |What it does

|*Show US States*
|Maps grid locators to US States and Canadian Provinces (useful without
JTAlert). Some grids span several states; then all are listed.

|*Enable Wait features*
|Turns *Wait and Reply*, *Wait and Call* and *Wait and Pounce* on (default).
See <<WAIT_FEATURES>>.

|*Clear DX Call after QSO* / *Clear DX Grid after QSO*
|Empties the DX Call / DX Grid box when the QSO is logged.

|*Show distance in messages with grid* / *Show azimuth in messages with grid*
|Adds distance and great-circle azimuth to every message with a valid locator.

|*Erase decodes after band change* / *Erase decodes after mode change*
|Clears Band Activity when you change band / mode.

|*Alternate Erase button behavior*
|*Erase* works like in JTDX: left-click clears Band Activity,
right-click clears Rx Frequency, double-click clears both.

|*Set Rx frequency to Tx frequency after QSO*
|After logging, Rx moves to your Tx frequency.

|*Band hopping every minute*
|With band hopping on, WS normally changes band every other minute.
Check this to change band every minute.

|*Align DXCC/distance/azimuth* and the two number boxes
|Lines country, distance and azimuth up in columns. The first box sets
the spaces between message and country (also without *Align*), the
second the spaces between country and distance/azimuth.
|===

NOTE: If you use a callsign with an add-on prefix or suffix, or want to
work such a station, read _Nonstandard Callsigns_ in the WSJT-X User Guide.

[[MAIN_WINDOW]]
== Main window

The main window of WS looks and works like the WSJT-X main window, with
extra buttons and more mouse actions. The WSJT-X User Guide describes the
standard controls. This chapter covers only what is new or different in
WS: everything you operate outside the menus and the *Settings* dialog.
menu:Help[Special mouse commands] gives a short summary.

[[WAIT_FEATURES]]
=== Wait features

The Wait features finish a QSO for you when the other station does not
answer at once or fades out in QSB. They all need *Auto Seq* checked and
*Enable Wait features* on the *General* tab (on by default, see
<<SETTINGS_GENERAL>>).

WARNING: With a Wait feature armed, a received message can switch your
rig to transmit without you touching anything. Keep your amplifier, ATU
and antenna ready, or disarm the feature.

[cols="2,5",options="header"]
|===
|Feature |How it works

|*Wait and Reply*
|Armed automatically while a callsign is in *DX Call* and *Enable Tx* is
off. When that station calls you (a message with your call and his call,
without 73), WS switches *Enable Tx* on and continues the QSO. It also
sends RR73 again if the other station did not copy the first one. Works
in FT2, FT4, FT8, FST4, JT4, JT9, JT65, MSK144 and Q65. Tx turns off
again after 8 periods (12 in MSK144).

|*Wait and Call*
|Click the *DX Call* button (it turns red). When the station in *DX Call*
reappears with CQ, RRR, RR73 or 73, WS calls him up to three times.
Works in FT2, FT4, FT8, FST4, MSK144 and Q65, in normal operation or as
FT8 Hound. Disarmed by *Halt Tx*, *Stop*, switching *Enable Tx* off,
clicking *DX Call* again, or as soon as the station answers you (the QSO
then continues normally).

|*Wait and Pounce*
|Select a *CQ:* mode other than *CQ: None* (see <<CQ_SELECTOR>>), then
right-click *Enable Tx* (it turns orange). WS answers the next CQ: the
first one, or the most distant, strongest or weakest station of the
period. Right-click *Enable Tx* again, or click *Halt Tx* or *Stop*, to
disarm. Not available as Fox.
|===

Wait and Pounce respects the filters. For example, WS can wait only for CQ
calls with a certain prefix, grid, callsign or keyword. With *Filters for
Wait & Pounce only* all decodes stay visible, but Wait and Pounce answers
only stations that pass the filters (see <<SETTINGS_FILTERS>>).

*Enable Tx* and *DX Call* turn yellow whenever a received message may
switch Tx on: with a callsign in *DX Call*, *Auto Seq* on and the Wait
features enabled, and always in Hound mode. Right-click *DX Call* to
clear *DX Call*, *DX Grid* and the standard messages; this disarms Wait
and Reply and Wait and Call at once.

[[CQ_SELECTOR]]
=== CQ: selector and Ignore List

The *CQ:* box next to *Auto Seq* is visible when *Auto Seq* is checked.

[cols="2,5",options="header"]
|===
|Selection |Result

|*CQ: None*
|Standard WSJT-X behaviour.

|*CQ: First*
|When you call CQ, WS answers the first station that calls you. With Wait
and Pounce, it answers the first CQ.

|*CQ: Max Dist*
|Picks the most distant station (a grid is needed to calculate the distance).

|*CQ: Max dB* / *CQ: Min dB*
|Picks the strongest / weakest station.
|===

The *Ignore* button (or kbd:[Ctrl+I]) puts the callsign in *DX Call* on
the Ignore List. With menu:Filters[Ignore stations from Ignore List]
checked, the *CQ:* modes (Wait and Pounce) skip these stations;
menu:Filters[Hide stations from Ignore List] removes them from Band
Activity. Clear the list with menu:File[Erase Ignore List]. See
<<MENU_FILTERS>> for the other *Ignore* and *Hide* options.

==== Callsigns on the waterfall

With menu:View[Callsigns on waterfall > Show decoded callsigns] (or *Show
Callsigns* in the Wide Graph), decoded calls appear as labels on the
waterfall at their frequency: red for the call in *DX Call*, green for
CQ, black for the rest. The *× TR* box sets how many periods a label
stays; *Clear* removes all labels. Left-click a label to put the call and
grid in *DX Call* / *DX Grid*, set Rx to that signal, set *Tx even/1st*
for the reply and generate the standard messages. Font, transparency and
position are in the *View* menu (see <<MENU_VIEW>>). In Q65,
menu:Decode[Disable clicks on waterfall] turns label clicks off.

==== Buttons

[cols="2,5",options="header"]
|===

[[BAND_BUTTONS]]
=== Band buttons

menu:View[Band Buttons] adds a row of band buttons, in all three GUI
variants. Click a button to go to the frequency of the current mode in
your frequency list. In FT8, FT4 and FT2, right-click 80 m to 10 m to go
to the FT8 DXpedition frequency of that band (6 m: FT8 only).
menu:View[VHF / UHF Buttons] switches the row to the bands from 8 m to
13 cm.

On 2 m, a right-click goes to 4 m (Regions 1 and 3) or 1.25 m (Region 2);
on 70 cm, a right-click goes to 23 cm.

[[MAIN_FILTERS]]
=== Filters in the main window

The status label at the bottom left shows when filters hide decodes: it
changes from green *Receiving* to cyan *Receiving, Filters On*. Check *BP*
(bypass) to switch all filters off for a while without losing their
settings; the label then reads *Receiving, Filters bypassed*. The filter
lists are set up on the *Filters* tab (<<SETTINGS_FILTERS>>); quick
filters are in the *Filters* menu (<<MENU_FILTERS>>).

[[BAND_ACTIVITY_INFO]]
=== Distance, azimuth and US States

If a message contains a valid locator, Band Activity can show the
distance and great-circle azimuth, lined up in columns with the country.
With *Show US States*, the US State or Canadian Province is shown too.
Turn these on and set the column spacing on the *General* tab
(<<SETTINGS_GENERAL>>).

[[CONTEST_NAME]]
=== Contest name and comments

With *CQ with individual contest name* and a *Contest name* of up to 4 letters (e.g. PACC)
on the *Advanced* tab, WS calls "CQ PACC" instead of "CQ TEST" or
"CQ RU" when a Special Operating Activity is active (FT modes, MSK144,
Q65; not as Fox or Hound).

With *Special operating activity to comments* on the *Reporting* tab
(<<SETTINGS_REPORTING>>), the *Log QSO* comment is filled with the
activity, e.g. "EU VHF Contest", "ARRL Field Day" or, with an individual
contest name, "PACC Contest". Retained comments and *dB reports to
comments* take priority.

[[LIVECQ_CHECKS]]
=== LiveCQ: who heard me

With *Enable Q65 LiveCQ* on the *Reporting* tab (<<SETTINGS_REPORTING>>)
and mode Q65, WS asks the LiveCQ server once per period which stations
have decoded your call. Those stations get a check mark and a bright
green frame on their waterfall label, and a check mark in the Active
Stations window in Q65 Pileup mode. So the DX can pick callers that
really copy him. The bottom of the Active Stations window shows
*LiveCQ connected* or a red *LiveCQ NO CONNECTION*.

[[TX_INHIBIT]]
=== TX Inhibit

With *Enable TX Inhibit* on the *Radio* tab (<<SETTINGS_RADIO>>), another
program can hold WS off the air while a priority radio on the same band
transmits. The status label then shows *Inhibit*.

[[WSMAP_CLICKS]]
=== Click-to-work from WS-MAP and EME65

With menu:Decode[Accept WS-MAP/EME65 click events] checked, clicking a
callsign in WS-MAP or EME65 sets *DX Call*, *DX Grid*, the frequency, the
Tx period and the submode in WS. A double-click there can also switch
*Enable Tx* on. This works in JT65 and Q65 with *Enable VHF and submode
features*. WS also processes the decodes forwarded by EME65.
Your *DX Call* and *DX Grid* are sent back, so WS-MAP or EME65 can follow
them.

If several WS instances watch the same source, click *Claim WS-MAP* in the
one that should act on clicks. Choose the source with
menu:Decode[WS-MAP/EME65 source] (see <<MENU_DECODE>>).

[[QSY_WINDOWS]]
=== Message Creator and QSY Monitor

These windows help you arrange a QSY with your QSO partner, for example
from an EME frequency to another band.

[cols="2,5",options="header"]
|===
|Window |What it does

|menu:View[Message Creator]
|Builds a short QSY request (band, mode and kHz) for the station in *DX
Call*, puts it in the free-text message and switches *Enable Tx* on.

|menu:View[Enable Message Popups]
|When a received message asks you to QSY, a popup shows the request and
lets you answer OKQSY or NOQSY.

|menu:View[QSY Monitor]
|Lists all QSY requests heard, with UTC, call, frequency and mode.
|===

[[DIAGNOSTIC_MODE]]
=== Diagnostic mode

Diagnostic mode collects data to find problems with WS or with its rig
control (Hamlib errors, COM ports that do not respond, and so on).

. Select menu:Save[Diagnostic mode].
. Close and restart WS, then reproduce the fault.
. Close WS as soon as the fault has appeared: the log files grow quickly.

WS creates a `logs` folder on your desktop (with a subfolder per
`--rig-name` instance) containing `ws_syslog.log` and `WS_RigControl.log`.
Open them with a text editor, or send them to the developers. Diagnostic
mode switches itself off the next time WS closes; to cancel it earlier,
select menu:Save[Default event logging].

[[DARK_STYLE]]
=== Dark style

menu:View[Use Dark Style] switches WS to a dark theme at once, without a
restart. It also adapts the waterfall lines and the button colours. With
dark style, choose softer (pastel) background colours on the *Colors* tab.
See <<MENU_VIEW>>.

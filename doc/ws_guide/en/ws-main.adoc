[preface]
== About this guide

WS is a suite of programs for weak-signal digital communication in
amateur radio: *WS* (the main program), *WS-MAP* (wideband Q65/FT8
monitor) and *EME65* (wideband JT65/Q65 for EME). WS grew out of
_WSJT-X_ and was formerly known as _WSJT-X Improved_. All modes and basic operating
procedures are the same as in WSJT-X; for those, the
https://wsjt.sourceforge.io/wsjtx-doc/wsjtx-main-3.0.0-rc1.html[WSJT-X User Guide]
remains the reference.

This guide is deliberately short. It explains every menu and every
setting of WS, and what WS adds to WSJT-X.
